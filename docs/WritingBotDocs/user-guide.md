# Writing Bot User Guide

## Getting Started

The Writing Bot is an AI-powered writing assistant designed to help students improve their essay writing skills through interactive feedback and guided revision.

**Access URL**: https://textbot.hkbu.tech/writingBot

## Initial Setup

### 1. API Configuration
Before using the Writing Bot, you need to configure your API connection:

1. Navigate to the **Briefing Mode** (default view)
2. Locate the "🔑 API Configuration" section
3. Get your API key from the [HKBU Generative AI Platform](https://genai.hkbu.edu.hk/settings/api-docs)
4. Paste your API key in the password field
5. Select your preferred AI model:
   - **GPT-5 Mini** (recommended for most users)
   - **GPT-5** (more advanced responses)
   - **GPT-4.1 Mini** (alternative option)
   - **GPT-4.1** (most advanced)
6. Click **"✅ Connect"**
7. Wait for the "✅ Connected and working!" confirmation

## Three Modes of Operation

### Briefing Mode
**Purpose**: Learn about assessment criteria and requirements

**Features**:
- View detailed rubrics for both assessment tasks
- Understand scoring criteria (1-5 point scales)
- Review task requirements and expectations
- Configure API settings

**How to Use**:
1. Read through Task 1 and Task 2 descriptions
2. Study the rubric tables to understand assessment criteria
3. Configure your API connection before proceeding

### Training Mode
**Purpose**: Practice essay revision with guided assistance

**Features**:
- Work with a teacher-provided sample essay
- Receive structured guidance from the AI assistant
- Track your progress with metrics
- Export your chat history

**How to Use**:
1. Click **"Training Mode"** after connecting your API
2. Review the sample essay provided
3. Start chatting with the AI assistant
4. Ask questions about specific areas:
   - Content & Ideas
   - Organisation & Structure
   - Vocabulary
   - Grammar & Sentence Structure
5. Follow the AI's suggestions and ask follow-up questions
6. Use the **"Export Chat History"** button to save your session

**Progress Tracking**:
- **Total Exchanges**: Number of back-and-forth conversations
- **Follow-up Questions**: Questions you asked for clarification
- **Revision Cycles**: Number of times you revised content

### Assessment Mode
**Purpose**: Independently revise your own essay draft

**Features**:
- Submit your original draft for analysis
- Work through revision process independently
- Compare original and final drafts side-by-side
- Generate improvement reports

**How to Use**:
1. Click **"Assessment Mode"**
2. Paste your original essay draft in the "Original Draft" text area
3. Click **"Confirm Original Draft"** (this cannot be undone)
4. Start chatting with the AI about your essay
5. Make revisions based on the conversation
6. Update your "Final Draft" with improvements
7. Click **"Confirm Final Draft & Generate Report"**

## Chat Interface Guide

### Starting a Conversation
- The AI will greet you with an appropriate message for your selected mode
- Type your questions or requests in the input field at the bottom
- Press **Enter** or click **"Send"** to submit your message

### Effective Communication Tips
1. **Be Specific**: Ask about particular paragraphs or sentences
2. **Ask Follow-up Questions**: Dig deeper into the AI's suggestions
3. **Request Examples**: Ask for specific examples of improvements
4. **Challenge Suggestions**: Question the AI's recommendations critically
5. **Iterate**: Keep refining based on the feedback received

### Sample Conversation Starters

**Training Mode**:
- "Can you help me improve the introduction of this essay?"
- "What are the main weaknesses in the sample essay's organization?"
- "How can I make the vocabulary more sophisticated?"
- "Are there any grammatical errors I should focus on?"

**Assessment Mode**:
- "Please review my essay and identify the main areas for improvement"
- "How can I strengthen my argument in the second paragraph?"
- "Is my conclusion effective? How can I improve it?"
- "What specific vocabulary changes would make this more academic?"

The Writing Bot helps you develop these key skills:

### 1. In-Depth Conversation

### 2. Critical Review
- Evaluate AI suggestions with a critical mindset
- Provide evidence-based justification for your choices

### 3. Iterative Refinement
- Go through multiple revision cycles
- Build on previous feedback systematically
- Show progressive improvement over time

## Assessment Rubric Understanding

### Content and Ideas (20%)
- **Excellent (5)**: Insightful, highly relevant ideas with compelling viewpoint
- **Proficient (4)**: Relevant, solid ideas with clear viewpoint and some depth
- **Developing (3)**: Relevant but basic ideas with weakly developed viewpoint
- **Basic (2)**: Somewhat related but vague ideas with unclear viewpoint
- **Limited (1)**: Irrelevant or minimally related ideas with no clear viewpoint

### Organisation and Logical Progression (20%)
- **Excellent (5)**: Highly organised with seamless, logical progression
- **Proficient (4)**: Well-organised with clear paragraphs and good flow
- **Developing (3)**: Clear structure but lacks depth or logical flow
- **Basic (2)**: Basic structure with unclear paragraphing
- **Limited (1)**: No clear structure, disjointed ideas

### Vocabulary (20%)
- **Excellent (5)**: Rich, precise vocabulary with masterful use of topic-specific terms
- **Proficient (4)**: Varied and precise vocabulary with effective topic-specific terms
- **Developing (3)**: Adequate vocabulary with some topic-specific terms
- **Basic (2)**: Basic vocabulary with minimal topic-specific terms
- **Limited (1)**: Limited, repetitive, or inaccurate vocabulary

### Grammar and Sentence Structure (20%)
- **Excellent (5)**: Virtually error-free with complex, varied sentences
- **Proficient (4)**: Minor errors with varied and mostly accurate sentences
- **Developing (3)**: Some errors with mostly correct but simple sentences
- **Basic (2)**: Several errors with simple and often flawed sentences
- **Limited (1)**: Frequent errors with incomplete or confusing sentences

## Best Practices

### For Training Mode
1. **Read the sample essay carefully** before starting the conversation
2. **Focus on one area at a time** (content, organization, vocabulary, or grammar)
3. **Ask specific questions** about particular sentences or paragraphs
4. **Request alternatives** for suggested improvements
5. **Document your learning** by exporting the chat history

### For Assessment Mode
1. **Prepare your draft** before starting the session
2. **Confirm your original draft** only when you're ready (cannot be changed)
3. **Engage deeply** with the AI's suggestions
4. **Make substantial revisions** based on the feedback
5. **Compare drafts carefully** before generating the final report

### General Tips
- **Be patient**: Take time to understand and implement suggestions
- **Stay engaged**: Don't just accept all suggestions without thinking
- **Ask why**: Understanding the reasoning behind suggestions is crucial
- **Practice regularly**: Consistent use will improve your skills
- **Export your work**: Save chat histories for later review

## Troubleshooting

### Connection Issues
- **Problem**: "❌ Failed to connect" message
- **Solution**: Check your API key and internet connection
- **Try**: Clear API settings and re-enter your key

### Chat Not Working
- **Problem**: Send button is disabled or messages don't appear
- **Solution**: Ensure you're connected to the API first
- **Check**: Connection status indicator should show "Connected"

### Export Problems
- **Problem**: Chat history export doesn't work
- **Solution**: Check your browser's download settings
- **Note**: Export saves as JSON format for later review

### Draft Confirmation Issues
- **Problem**: Cannot confirm original draft
- **Solution**: Ensure you have text in the original draft field
- **Warning**: Confirmation cannot be undone in the same session

## Privacy and Data Security

- **API Keys**: Stored locally in your browser, not on servers
- **Chat History**: Not permanently stored, only exists during your session
- **Export Data**: Downloaded directly to your device
- **Privacy**: Your conversations are not monitored or saved by the system
