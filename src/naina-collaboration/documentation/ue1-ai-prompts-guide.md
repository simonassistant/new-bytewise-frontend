# UE1-Specific AI Prompts and Use Cases

## Contextualized Prompts for University English 1

### **Writing Development Prompts**

#### Essay Structure and Organization
```
"I'm writing a 5-paragraph argumentative essay for UE1 about [topic]. Help me:
1. Develop a clear thesis statement that takes a position
2. Create topic sentences for each body paragraph
3. Ensure my argument flows logically from introduction to conclusion

Please focus on structure, not content generation."
```

#### Academic Paragraph Development
```
"Review this paragraph from my UE1 essay and help me improve the:
- Topic sentence clarity
- Evidence integration
- Analysis depth
- Transition to next paragraph

[Insert student's paragraph here]"
```

#### Thesis Statement Refinement
```
"I'm working on a thesis statement for my UE1 research paper. My current thesis is: '[student's thesis]'
Help me evaluate if it:
- Makes a clear argument
- Is specific enough for a university-level paper
- Can be supported with evidence
- Follows UE1 thesis statement guidelines"
```

### **Research and Critical Thinking Prompts**

#### Source Evaluation
```
"I found this source for my UE1 research project: [source details]
Help me evaluate:
- Author credentials and expertise
- Publication credibility
- Relevance to my research question
- Potential bias or limitations
- How it fits UE1 standards for academic sources"
```

#### Reading Comprehension Support
```
"I'm reading this passage for UE1: [insert passage]
Help me:
- Identify the main argument
- Understand complex vocabulary in context
- Recognize the author's tone and purpose
- Prepare questions for class discussion

Please don't summarize for me, but guide my understanding."
```

#### Research Question Development
```
"I need to develop a research question for my UE1 final project on [broad topic].
Help me:
- Narrow down to a specific, arguable question
- Ensure it's appropriate for university-level research
- Consider what sources I'll need to answer it
- Frame it for a UE1 academic audience"
```

### **Language and Grammar Development**

#### Academic Writing Style
```
"Review this sentence from my UE1 essay for academic tone and clarity:
'[student's sentence]'

Help me:
- Make it more academically appropriate
- Improve clarity and concision
- Maintain my voice while enhancing sophistication
- Follow UE1 style guidelines"
```

#### Citation and Integration Practice
```
"I want to use this quote in my UE1 essay: '[quote]' from [source]
Help me:
- Integrate it smoothly into my paragraph
- Provide proper context and introduction
- Follow [MLA/APA] citation format correctly
- Analyze rather than just insert the quote"
```

#### Vocabulary Enhancement
```
"I'm writing about [topic] for UE1. Help me find more precise, academic vocabulary for these words/phrases:
[list basic terms]

Suggest alternatives that:
- Fit university-level writing
- Maintain clarity for my audience
- Are appropriate for the context"
```

### **Revision and Editing Prompts**

#### Draft Review
```
"I've completed a draft of my UE1 [assignment type]. Please help me identify:
- Areas where my argument could be stronger
- Paragraphs that need better organization
- Sentences that could be clearer
- Transitions that need improvement

[Insert draft or specific sections]"
```

#### Peer Review Preparation
```
"I'm preparing to peer review a classmate's UE1 essay. Help me understand:
- What to look for in effective academic arguments
- How to provide constructive feedback
- Questions to ask about thesis and evidence
- Ways to comment on organization and clarity"
```

## UE1-Aligned Use Cases

### **Appropriate AI Assistance**
1. **Brainstorming and Planning**
   - Topic exploration for assignments
   - Outline organization
   - Research direction guidance

2. **Language Support**
   - Grammar and style feedback
   - Vocabulary suggestions
   - Sentence structure improvement

3. **Research Guidance**
   - Source evaluation criteria
   - Citation format help
   - Research strategy development

4. **Revision Support**
   - Identifying weak arguments
   - Improving paragraph structure
   - Enhancing clarity and flow

### **Inappropriate AI Use**
1. **Content Generation**
   - Writing essays or paragraphs
   - Creating arguments or analysis
   - Generating research content

2. **Assignment Completion**
   - Completing homework independently
   - Taking online quizzes/tests
   - Submitting AI-generated work

3. **Research Shortcuts**
   - Using AI as a primary source
   - Avoiding reading assignments
   - Replacing library research

## Implementation Guidelines for UE1 Instructors

### **Teaching AI Literacy**
1. **Demonstrate appropriate prompts** in class
2. **Practice AI interaction** as a learning activity
3. **Discuss AI limitations** and verification needs
4. **Model transparent AI use** in teaching

### **Assessment Considerations**
1. **Clear AI use policies** in syllabi
2. **AI disclosure requirements** for assignments
3. **Focus on process documentation** in portfolios
4. **Evaluate critical thinking** over final products

### **Student Support**
1. **Workshops on effective prompting**
2. **Guidelines for AI-assisted research**
3. **Practice sessions with feedback**
4. **Peer learning opportunities**

## Quality Control Questions for AI Responses

Students should always ask themselves:
1. **Accuracy**: Is this information correct and current?
2. **Relevance**: Does this apply to my specific UE1 assignment?
3. **Appropriateness**: Is this the right academic level and tone?
4. **Completeness**: What additional verification do I need?
5. **Ethics**: Am I using this assistance appropriately?

---

*Note: These prompts are designed to enhance learning while maintaining academic integrity. Students should always verify AI responses with credible sources and follow their institution's AI use policies.*
